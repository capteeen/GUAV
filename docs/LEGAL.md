# LEGAL.md — Guava

This note is not legal advice. It is adapted from the Guava kit so the repository states what this product is.

## Original brand

- Product name: **Guava** / **Gua**
- Site: **gua.fun**
- Ticker: **GUAV**
- Board: **The Grove**
- Tagline: **Ripe launches. Fresh pairs.**

Guava is an original launchpad. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a fork of any other launchpad, wallet scanner, or fruit-named crypto product. Do not treat unrelated mints that use a similar word as this brand.

Product shape (a discovery board, a launch form, and an in-app trade panel) was studied from a public memepad, avo.fun. Guava uses its own name, palette, mark, and program.

## What this phase ships

- Solana devnet demo
- A bonding curve quoted in SOL
- Graduation into a Guava-owned constant-product pool with LP locked inside this program
- Platform fee of 100 bps and a creator fee of 0–500 bps

## What this phase does not ship

- Mainnet
- Stock or USDC pairing on the curve
- A claim of audited financial figures
- Affiliation with any third-party exchange or chain brand

Market rows labeled demo data are samples. Live rows, when a program id is configured and accounts exist, are read from Guava accounts.

## Licenses

Guava’s own program and app in this repository are MIT, except where a dependency keeps its own license. Anchor and anchor-spl are Apache-2.0. This repo does not vendor another launchpad’s program id or bytecode.

Memecoins can go to zero. A launchpad can have regulatory exposure depending on where it is offered. This document does not provide compliance guidance.
