# Guava placeholder mark

The mark in `public/logo.svg` and `app/icon.svg` is a temporary geometric cross-section: green rind, pink flesh, a few cream seeds. It is not a finished mascot.

Hand the illustrator this brief (from the Guava kit):

- Mark: stylized guava cross-section — green rind ring, pink flesh annulus, small cream seed cluster at the center. Geometric, two or three flat colors, readable at 16×16.
- Wordmark: “Guava” title case, optional small “gua.fun” undermark in mono.
- Optional later mascot: a whole guava with a tiny leaf crown. Not a pear-shaped pit fruit. No copied marks from other launchpads.
- UI icons: seed for a new token, a cut for the launch preview, a grove grid for the board, a droplet for fees, a lock for LP.

Palette to keep: `#0B140F`, `#122018`, `#1F3D2C`, `#2F6B3A`, `#3F9A4E`, `#F28CA0`, `#D45B78`, `#F6F1E4`, `#B7B0A0`, `#FFE08A`, `#E85D5D`.

Type: DM Sans for UI, IBM Plex Mono for tickers and fees, Space Grotesk for the hero only.
